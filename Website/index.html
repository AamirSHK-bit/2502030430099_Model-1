Our Story

Born from a Love of Adventure

Founded in 2009 in Ahmedabad, Wanderlust began as one traveller's obsession with crafting perfect journeys. Today, we're a global team of 200+ passionate explorers who believe travel changes lives.

Our Mission

Travel That Transforms

We believe that every journey should be more than a trip — it should be a transformation. Our mission is to create experiences that connect people to the world's most extraordinary places, cultures, and stories.

From our office in Ahmedabad to our partners across 6 continents, every member of the Wanderlust family shares one goal: to make your travel dreams come true.
